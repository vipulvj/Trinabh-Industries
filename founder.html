Her Story

Architect. Designer.Visionary.

Kirti Pandey is a certified architect registered with the Council of Architecture, India — bringing formal architectural training, Vastu wisdom and deep interior design expertise to every project at Trinabh Industries.

With over a decade of hands-on experience across high-end residential and commercial interiors, she has collaborated with prominent names in the industry — delivering spaces for corporate offices, luxury homes, retail showrooms and institutional projects across Delhi NCR.

Her approach integrates modern architecture with Vastu Shastra principles — creating spaces that are not only visually refined but harmonious, efficient and deeply liveable. At Trinabh Industries, she bridges design intelligence with factory-floor precision, ensuring every piece reflects genuine design intent.

Vastu Expertise

Design That Breathes

Kirti integrates Vastu Shastra with modern architecture — applied spatial planning that considers orientation, flow, material choice and energy alignment at every stage of design.

Clients receive spaces designed to feel right, not just look right. This is particularly valued by families buying new homes who want a holistic first interior experience.

  • ◆Room orientation & layout analysis before design
  • ◆Kitchen placement and cooking direction alignment
  • ◆Material & colour choices aligned with spatial energy
  • ◆Entry & flow design for positive energy movement
  • ◆Remedial suggestions for existing layouts
Sister Firm

Studio Antarit

Kirti also leads Studio Antarit — an independent design and Vastu practice focused on concept development, spatial planning and holistic design solutions.

While Trinabh Industries handles manufacturing and installation, Studio Antarit focuses on upstream design — 3D visualisation, space planning, material curation and Vastu consultation.

Together they offer a genuinely end-to-end experience: intelligent design from Studio Antarit, precision manufacturing from Trinabh Industries.
