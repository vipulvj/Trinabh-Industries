Who We Are

Factory-Direct.
Zero Middlemen.

Trinabh Industries is a premium modular furniture manufacturer and interior design firm based in Sahibabad Industrial Area, Ghaziabad. We serve residential and commercial clients across Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad and all of Delhi NCR.

Founded in 2012 and led by Kirti Pandey — a certified architect registered with the Council of Architecture, India, and National Women of Excellence Award 2017 recipient — Trinabh Industries operates a manufacturing factory at A 16/23, Site IV, Sahibabad Industrial Area, Ghaziabad (PIN 201010). The company manufactures modular kitchens (starting ₹1.5 lakhs), custom wardrobes (starting ₹75,000), walk-in closets, office workstations, TV units, study units, and pooja units using BWR-grade plywood, CNC panel saws, and hardware from global brands Hettich and Blum. Operating factory-direct with no dealer markup, Trinabh Industries serves all of Delhi NCR and provides free site visits, 3D design consultation, and a 1-year installation warranty on all projects.

We specialise in modern office interiors — modular workstations, glass cabins, and complete turnkey office fit-outs — and for homes, we design and manufacture modular kitchens, custom wardrobes, walk-in closets and TV units, tailored to maximise space, style and everyday usability.

Our factory-direct manufacturing approach means you deal directly with the manufacturer — transparent pricing, faster delivery, better quality control, and no retailer markup. We use premium hardware from Hettich and Blum — the global standard for modular furniture.

Our Philosophy

Why Factory-Direct Matters

Most modular furniture in India passes through 2–3 layers of middlemen before reaching the customer — each adding margin, each diluting accountability. When something goes wrong, no one owns the problem.

At Trinabh Industries, we are the manufacturer. We design, produce, deliver and install everything in-house. You get manufacturer pricing, manufacturer accountability, and manufacturer support — directly.

This means: faster response when you have questions. Direct resolution when you have issues. And pricing that reflects the actual cost of production — not four layers of markup.

Our quality promise:

  • ◆BWR-grade (Boiling Water Resistant) plywood core — standard on every piece
  • ◆Hettich or Blum hardware — industry's most trusted brands, globally
  • ◆Automatic edge banding — zero gaps, zero lifting, 10+ year finish life
  • ◆Full 3D design consultation before any production begins
  • ◆Post-installation support — we come back if anything needs adjustment
