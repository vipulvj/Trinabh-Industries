Common Questions

Wardrobe FAQs

How much does a built-in wardrobe cost in Ghaziabad? +

Built-in wardrobes in Ghaziabad typically start from ₹80,000 for a basic 6-foot wardrobe and go up to ₹3–5 lakhs for a full walk-in closet with premium finishes and LED lighting.

How long does wardrobe installation take? +

A standard built-in wardrobe takes 1–2 days to install. A full walk-in closet room typically takes 3–4 days.

Can you build floor-to-ceiling wardrobes? +

Absolutely — floor-to-ceiling wardrobes are our speciality. They maximise storage and look stunning. We customise to any ceiling height.

What finish options are available? +

We offer high-gloss acrylic, matte laminate, wood veneer, mirror, fluted glass, frosted glass and PU paint finishes. Hundreds of colours and textures available.
