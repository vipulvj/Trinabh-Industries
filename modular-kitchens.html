Kitchen Types We Build

Every Layout, Perfected

🔲
Straight / Linear Kitchen
Ideal for compact spaces. Clean, efficient single-wall design with maximum storage. Perfect for 2BHK and studio apartments.
📐
L-Shape Kitchen
The most popular layout — uses two adjacent walls for a natural work triangle. Excellent traffic flow and counter space.
🔳
U-Shape Kitchen
Maximum storage and counter space on three walls. Perfect for large kitchens and serious home cooks.
🏝️
Island Kitchen
A freestanding central island adds prep space, seating and style. The luxury kitchen choice for open-plan homes.
↔️
Parallel / Galley
Two parallel counters facing each other — ultra-efficient for narrow spaces. Popular in apartments.
🪟
Peninsula Kitchen
An attached island on one end — gives island benefits without needing a fully open floor plan.

A modular kitchen starts from ₹1.5 lakhs for a basic straight kitchen and goes up to ₹8–10 lakhs for a premium island kitchen. Trinabh Industries offers factory-direct pricing — cutting retailer margins.

How long does installation take? +

From design sign-off to installation: 3–4 weeks total. Site installation takes 3–5 working days depending on kitchen size.

Yes! We serve Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and all of Delhi NCR.
