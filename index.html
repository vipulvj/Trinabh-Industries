About Trinabh Industries

Factory-Direct Quality.
Zero Compromise.

Trinabh Industries is a premium modular furniture manufacturer and interior design firm based in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad — serving residential and commercial clients across Delhi NCR.


We specialise in modern office interiors, modular workstations, glass cabins and complete turnkey office fit-outs, creating functional and aesthetically refined workspaces for growing businesses. For homes, we design and manufacture modular kitchens, custom wardrobes, walk-in closets and TV units — tailored to maximise space, style and everyday usability.


Our factory-direct manufacturing facility ensures precision-engineered furniture using automatic panel saw and edge-banding technology — delivering flawless finish, durability and consistent quality. We use premium hardware from trusted brands like Hettich and Blum.


Serving Delhi · Noida · Gurgaon · Faridabad · Ghaziabad · Indirapuram · Vasundhara · Vaishali · Raj Nagar · Greater Noida — end-to-end, factory to installation, without middlemen.

Know Our Founder

Kirti Pandey
CEO & Principal Architect
◆ Council of Architecture — Certified Architect
◆ National Women of Excellence Award 2017
◆ International Publications & Research
Sister Design Firm
Studio Antarit → 📸 @studioantarit on Instagram

Kirti Pandey, CEO of Trinabh Industries, is a certified architect registered with the Council of Architecture, India — bringing a rare and powerful blend of architecture, Vastu wisdom and spatial design expertise to every project she undertakes.

With over a decade of hands-on experience across high-end residential and commercial interiors, Kirti has collaborated with prominent names in the industry — delivering meticulously crafted spaces for corporate offices, luxury homes, retail showrooms and institutional spaces across Delhi NCR and beyond.

Her design philosophy goes beyond aesthetics — she seamlessly integrates modern architecture with Vastu Shastra principles to create spaces that are not only visually refined, but harmonious, efficient and deeply liveable. This holistic approach has made her a trusted name for clients who want their spaces to truly perform and inspire.

Kirti was honoured with the prestigious National Women of Excellence Award in 2017, in recognition of her outstanding contribution to design and innovation. Her work and research have been featured in leading national and international publications — including an architectural study on the conurbation of Zari craftsmen at Varanasi — reflecting her deep interest in the intersection of craft, culture and contemporary design.

"Our vision is to combine precision manufacturing with intelligent design — to create spaces that truly perform and inspire."

— Kirti Pandey, CEO, Trinabh Industries

Kirti also leads the design and Vastu practice through her sister concern, Studio Antarit — an independent design studio focused on concept development, spatial planning and holistic design solutions for discerning clients.
