Study Unit Questions

How much does a custom study unit cost? +

A custom integrated study unit in Ghaziabad starts from around ₹40,000 for a basic desk with overheads and goes up to ₹1.5–2 lakhs for a full library-study wall with LED lighting.

Can you build a study unit in a small bedroom? +

Absolutely — we specialise in making the most of compact spaces. We'll design a study unit that fits your room perfectly without making it feel cramped.

Do study units come with lighting? +

Yes — we can integrate Philips LED strips under overhead cabinets and ambient lighting within display sections. Excellent for reducing eye strain during long study sessions.
