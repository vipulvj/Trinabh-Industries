The Real Cost of Office Interiors in Delhi NCR (2026)

📋 Related: See our Complete 2026 Office Interior Cost Guide → with detailed per-sq-ft breakdowns and category-wise budgets.

Planning an office interior in Delhi NCR but unsure what it will cost? You're not alone. Most business owners get wildly varying quotes — from ₹800 per sq ft to ₹4,000+ per sq ft — and have no idea what drives the difference. This guide breaks it down clearly.

Key Takeaway: A well-designed office interior in Delhi NCR costs between ₹1,200–₹2,800 per sq ft for mid-range, and ₹3,000–₹5,000+ for premium. The biggest cost driver is furniture quality and whether you use a manufacturer or a retailer.

Why Factory-Direct Saves 25–40%

Most office furniture vendors in Delhi NCR are retailers — they buy from manufacturers (like us) and add a 30–50% markup. When you work directly with a manufacturer like Trinabh Industries, you get the same — often better — quality at factory price.

Example: A 20-seat office workstation setup that costs ₹8 lakhs through a retailer typically costs ₹5–6 lakhs when ordered directly from the manufacturer. That's ₹2–3 lakhs saved on a single project.

Ghaziabad vs Noida vs Gurgaon — Cost Differences

Labour and installation costs vary slightly by location, but since furniture is manufactured at a central factory, the product cost is the same regardless of where you're located in Delhi NCR.

  • Ghaziabad / Noida: Slightly lower civil and labour costs. Good infrastructure. 2–3 week delivery from our Sahibabad factory.
  • Gurgaon / South Delhi: Higher labour costs for civil work. Same furniture pricing. Factor in 10–15% higher for overall project.
  • Central Delhi / CP: Premium zone — expect civil and labour costs to be 20–30% higher, but furniture cost remains same.

How to Get the Best Value

Follow these steps to get the best office interior in your budget:

  1. Get 3 quotes — one from a retailer, one from a turnkey vendor, one directly from a manufacturer. The difference will surprise you.
  2. Ask to see the factory — any legitimate manufacturer will invite you to visit. If they won't show you where it's made, walk away.
  3. Insist on 3D design before signing — see exactly what you're getting before you commit.
  4. Specify hardware brands — insist on Hettich, Blum or equivalent for anything with moving parts. Unknown brands fail within 1–2 years.
  5. Check plywood grade — BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) marine ply only. MDF swells and fails in air-conditioned offices.
