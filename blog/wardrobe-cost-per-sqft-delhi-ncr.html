Wardrobe Cost Per Square Foot in Delhi NCR — 2026 Guide

Wardrobe pricing is one of the most confusing areas in modular furniture. Vendors quote per running foot, per square foot, per module, or lump sum — making comparisons nearly impossible. This guide standardises everything so you can compare quotes fairly.

Reference Rate: A standard laminate wardrobe in Delhi NCR costs ₹850–1,400 per square foot of wardrobe face area (height × width). Sliding door wardrobes: ₹1,200–2,200 per sq ft. Walk-in closets: ₹1,500–3,500 per sq ft depending on fittings.

What Affects Wardrobe Price Most

  • Internal fittings: Every drawer, trouser rack, saree rack, and pull-out adds ₹2,000–6,000. A fully fitted master wardrobe has 8–12 fittings.
  • Mirror panels: Full-length mirrors on doors add ₹3,000–8,000 per shutter depending on mirror type.
  • Loft above wardrobe: Often forgotten — adds ₹15,000–35,000 to seal the gap to ceiling.
  • Ply thickness: 18mm BWR ply (standard) vs 19mm or 25mm (premium). Don't let vendors downgrade without telling you.
  • Hardware brand: Genuine Hettich soft-close hinges and channels vs unknown brands — costs ₹400–800 more per wardrobe but lasts 3× longer.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

  1. Is this price per sq ft of face area (height × width) or floor area?
  2. What ply thickness and BWR grade are you using?
  3. Which hardware brand and can you show me their catalogue number?
  4. Is installation included? What about the loft/storage above?
  5. What is the warranty on structure vs hardware?
