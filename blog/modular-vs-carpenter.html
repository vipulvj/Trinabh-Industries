Modular Office Furniture vs Carpenter Work — The Honest Comparison

When setting up an office in Delhi NCR, one of the first questions every business owner asks is: should I go with modular furniture or hire a carpenter to build everything? Having manufactured office furniture for 12+ years, here's our honest, unbiased breakdown.

Short Answer: For offices, modular furniture wins in almost every category — quality, consistency, speed and long-term value. Carpenter work made sense 15 years ago. Today, factory-manufactured modular furniture is better quality AND often cheaper.

Warranty✅ Manufacturer warranty❌ No formal warranty

The Hidden Cost of Carpenter Work

Many businesses choose carpenter work because the initial quote looks cheaper. But here's what typically happens:

  • Quote creep: Carpenter quotes increase by 20–40% during the job as "unexpected" costs appear
  • Material quality: Without your supervision, cheaper materials get used
  • Time overrun: 6-week projects become 12-week projects, blocking your office setup
  • Maintenance costs: Hand-pasted edge banding peels within 1–2 years in AC offices
  • No recourse: If something goes wrong, there's no warranty to fall back on

When Carpenter Work Still Makes Sense

We're honest about this. Carpenter work can still be the right choice for:

  • Very small, one-off custom pieces with unusual dimensions
  • Highly ornate or carved decorative elements
  • Extremely tight budgets where any saving matters
  • Remote locations where delivery is impractical

For standard office furniture — workstations, cabinets, reception counters, conference tables — modular is almost always the better choice.
